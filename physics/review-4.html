Frequency

Cycles per second

Measured in hertz. Diagnostic ultrasound commonly uses megahertz.

Period

Time for one complete cycle

Measured in seconds or microseconds.

Core Relationship

Frequency and period are reciprocals.

When one increases, the other decreases.

Explore the relationship

Slide from 1 MHz to 15 MHz. As frequency rises, more cycles fit into the same time window and each individual cycle takes less time.

Live wave representation

The horizontal axis represents time. The gold wave changes as the frequency slider moves. The red bracket identifies one complete period.

The bracket spans from one identical point on the wave to the next identical point.

What to notice

  • The horizontal axis is time.
  • Frequency tells how many cycles occur each second.
  • Period tells how long one cycle takes.
  • Higher frequency creates more tightly packed cycles.
  • Higher frequency produces a shorter period.
  • Lower frequency produces a longer period.
Memory rule: f ↑ means T ↓, and f ↓ means T ↑.
