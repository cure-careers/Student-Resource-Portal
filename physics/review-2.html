Pulsed Ultrasound Parameters

Understand what each parameter measures and how depth changes the pulse sequence.

Pulse: a collection of cycles with a beginning and an end.
Pulse Duration: time from the start to the end of a pulse. Listening time is not included.
Spatial Pulse Length: physical length of a pulse in space.
PRP: start of one pulse to the start of the next.
PRF: number of pulses per second.
Duty Factor: fraction of time the system is transmitting.

Essential Equations

PD = Period × Cycles
SPL = Cycles × Wavelength
PRF = 1 ÷ PRP
DF = PD ÷ PRP

Intensity Fundamentals

Recognize the definitions, ranking, and conversion rules that commonly appear on SPI questions.

Power

Total energy in the wave.

Units: watts

Relationship: Power ∝ amplitude²

Intensity

Power divided by area.

Units: W/cm²

Equation: Intensity = Power/Area

Spatial & Temporal

Spatial describes beam location. Temporal describes time.

Intensity Ranking

TypeMeaningSPI Recognition

Pulse Average → Temporal Average

SPPA × DF = SPTA

Spatial Peak → Spatial Average

SPTA ÷ SP/SA = SATA

Beam Uniformity Coefficient describes spatial distribution and is ≥ 1.

Attenuation, Decibels, and Impedance

Connect sound weakening, intensity ratios, and reflection.

Attenuation

Weakening of sound with depth, expressed as negative decibels.

Attenuation coefficient: dB/cm

Major cause in soft tissue: absorption, about 80%.

Impedance

Z = Density × Speed

Units are Rayls. A greater impedance mismatch produces greater reflection.

Range Equation and Timing Rules

Use time of flight to determine reflector depth.

Range Equation

Depth = (Speed × Time) ÷ 2

The division by 2 accounts for the round trip.

13 Microsecond Rule

Every 13 μs of round-trip time corresponds to 1 cm of depth.

39 μs corresponds to 3 cm.

Doppler Essentials

Review angle, aliasing, Nyquist, and CW versus PW.

Doppler Angle

Measured Velocity = True Velocity × cos θ

Aliasing Essentials

Nyquist Limit = PRF ÷ 2

Aliasing occurs in pulsed wave Doppler when the Doppler shift exceeds the Nyquist limit.

  • Increase PRF or use a shallower depth.
  • Use a lower transmit frequency.
  • Use CW Doppler.
